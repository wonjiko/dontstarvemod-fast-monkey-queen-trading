# Fast Monkey Queen Trading

Give the Queen of Moon Quay your next Banana as soon as she finishes processing the previous one. Her receiving and curse-preparation animations run at 8× speed, and curse tokens return faster. You no longer need to wait through her happy or curse-result reaction before trading again.

Erase Blueprints into Papyrus at a Cartographer's Desk with a short action instead of the long crafting animation. Other paper-erasing actions keep their original timing.

The Queen still receives each Banana normally. The original game controls item consumption, blueprint rewards, removal of up to four Monkey Curse stacks, and right of passage. Her receiving animation and curse channel retain their original completion events. Starting another trade can interrupt the final reaction animation.

Blueprint erasing still uses the original game action: one Blueprint is consumed and Papyrus is given as usual.

**Multiplayer:** Enable the mod on the server and on every joining client.

**Compatibility:** Tested against the game scripts from build 756039. In-game multiplayer verification is pending.
