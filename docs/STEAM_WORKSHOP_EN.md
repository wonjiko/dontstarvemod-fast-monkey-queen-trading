# Fast Monkey Queen Trading

Give the Queen of Moon Quay your next Banana as soon as she finishes processing the previous one. You no longer need to wait through her happy or curse-result reaction before trading again.

The Queen still receives each Banana normally. The original game controls item consumption, blueprint rewards, removal of up to four Monkey Curse stacks, and right of passage. Her receiving animation and curse channel are preserved. Starting another trade can interrupt the final reaction animation.

**Multiplayer:** Enable the mod on the server and on every joining client.

**Compatibility:** Tested against the game scripts from build 756039. In-game multiplayer verification is pending.

Source: https://github.com/wonjiko/dontstarvemod-fast-monkey-queen-trading
